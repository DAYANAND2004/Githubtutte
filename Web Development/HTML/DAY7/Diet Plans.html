Exercises

Exercise keeps your body healthy, strong, and active.

1. Bench Press

Great exercise for building chest strength and size.

2. Pull Down

Effective for building a strong and wide back.

3. Squats

Builds lower body strength and improves endurance.

4. Shoulder Press

Targets shoulder muscles and improves upper body strength.

5. Bicep Curls

Isolates the biceps and helps in building arm size.

6. Tricep Dips

Effective for building stronger triceps.

7. Deadlifts

Builds overall strength and targets multiple muscle groups.

8. Cardio

Improves heart health and helps in fat burning.
