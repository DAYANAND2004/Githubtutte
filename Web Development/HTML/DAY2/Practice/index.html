Cyber Security Basics

Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, and data from cyber attacks, hackers, and viruses. It helps keep personal and business information safe from unauthorized access

Cyber security is important because it protects privacy, prevents data theft, and ensures that online services remain safe and reliable.

Why is Cyber Security Important?

  1. Protects personal information
  2. Prevents hacking and data theft
  3. Keeps online banking secure
  4. Protects businesses from cyber attacks
  5. Ensures safe internet browsing

Basic Cyber Security Tips

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
  • Keep your software updated.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious links.
  • Install trusted antivirus software.
  • Back up important files regularly.
